# Visual Directive: Subtle Neon, Simple, Native — Founder (2026-10-11)

> "Think modern, subtle neon inspired borders and sparks, but simple and easy on the eyes, user friendly, natively intuitive to the average US Citizen to help with their day to day priorities."

## The signature element: subtle neon borders + sparks
- Borders that glow softly; tiny spark accents. **SUBTLE is the law.**
- The difference between a firefly and a floodlight. Firefly wins.
- Neon guides the eye to what matters — it is never decoration for its own sake.

## Restful, not stimulating
- No visual noise. No competing glows. Generous whitespace.
- A tired parent at 11pm finds it restful. If it stimulates, it fails.
- Animation budget (from FRONTEND-CONSTRAINTS.md) is a ceiling, not a target.

## Natively intuitive to the average US citizen
- Not tech workers, not designers. Facebook-and-email fluency is the bar.
- 5-second rule: if they can't figure it out in 5 seconds, it's wrong.
- No jargon. No clever-but-confusing patterns. Familiar shapes, honest labels.

## Day-to-day priorities first
- Visual hierarchy serves "what do I need to handle today."
- The neon accents point at priorities, not features.
- Never "look at all our features."

## The filter (gate question for every visual review)
> Does this feel like a **calm, glowing garden that shows me my day** — or a **tech product showing off**?

If the latter: KILL.
